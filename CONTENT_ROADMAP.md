# Content Expansion Guide

Based on the E:\LIBRARY materials, here are suggestions for expanding the PlasticSurgery Study Center content:

## Priority Modules to Add

### 1. Hand Surgery
**Files:** 100 Hand Cases.pdf, contractures hand post burn.pdf, Syndactyly Release.pdf
**Content:**
- Hand trauma management
- Tendon injuries and repair
- Nerve injuries (median, ulnar, radial)
- Flexor and extensor tendon anatomy
- Syndactyly classification and release
- Post-burn hand contractures

### 2. Microsurgery & Free Flaps
**Files:** Practical Guide to Free Tissue Transfer.pdf, Perforator Flaps for Breast Reconstruction.pdf, PERFORATOR FLAPS.pdf
**Content:**
- Microsurgical principles
- Free flap selection
- DIEP, SIEA, GAP, TUG flaps
- Perforator anatomy
- Flap monitoring
- Complication management

### 3. Facial Reconstruction
**Files:** Atlas of Minimally Invasive Facelift.pdf, Functional Reconstructive Nasal Surgery.pdf, Ear reconstruction.pdf
**Content:**
- Facial nerve anatomy
- Facial trauma
- Nasal reconstruction
- Ear reconstruction (microtia)
- Facial reanimation
- Skin cancer reconstruction

### 4. Aesthetic Surgery
**Files:** Cosmetic Facial Surgery.pdf, ART OF AESTHETIC SURGERY.pdf, Atlas of Liposuction.pdf, BREAST AND RHINOPLASTY.pdf
**Content:**
- Rhinoplasty techniques
- Blepharoplasty
- Facelift approaches
- Liposuction principles
- Breast augmentation
- Body contouring

### 5. Oncoplastic Surgery
**Files:** The role of Oncoplastic Breast Surgery.pdf, final_oncoplastic_guidelines_for_use.pdf
**Content:**
- Oncoplastic principles
- Volume displacement techniques
- Volume replacement techniques
- Contralateral symmetry
- Radiation considerations

### 6. Pressure Ulcers & Wound Care
**Files:** Chapters_Pressure Ulcer.pdf, Vacuum-Assisted Wound Closure.pdf
**Content:**
- Pressure ulcer classification
- Prevention strategies
- Surgical management
- Flap coverage options
- NPWT applications

### 7. Skin Cancer & Mohs Surgery
**Files:** Multiple references
**Content:**
- BCC, SCC, melanoma
- Mohs micrographic surgery

- Sentinel lymph node biopsy

### 8. Lymphedema Surgery
**Files:** 2014 Ann Surg Oncol Granzow Lymphedema Surgery Review.pdf, surgical-treatment-modalities-for-lymphedema.pdf
**Content:**
- Lymphedema pathophysiology
- Classification systems
- Microsurgical treatment (LVA)
- VLNT (vascularized lymph node transfer)
- Liposuction for lymphedema

### 9. Chest Wall Reconstruction
**Files:** chest wall tumors.pdf, chest wall.pdf, InTech-Thoracic_reconstruction.pdf
**Content:**
- Chest wall anatomy
- Tumor resection principles
- Reconstruction options
- Pectoralis major flap
- Latissimus dorsi flap

### 10. Distraction Osteogenesis
**Files:** 02 Distraction Osteogenesis - IJD 2012 Oct - Dec.pdf, InTech-Distraction_osteogenesis.pdf

### 11. Penile & Urogenital Reconstruction
**Files:** Fracture of the Penis.pdf, Management of Penile Fracture.pdf, female cosmetic genital surgery.pdf
**Content:**

### 12. Flap Surgery Principles
**Files:** Flaps practical reconstructive surgery.pdf, Practical Techniques in Flap Surgery.pdf, Principles of delay.pdf
**Content:**
- Random vs axial pattern flaps
- Local flaps (rotation, transposition, advancement)
- Regional flaps
- Free flaps
- Flap delay principles
- Z-plasty techniques

## MCQ Assessment Topics to Expand

### For Each New Module:
- 50 questions minimum
- Mix of easy (30%), medium (50%), hard (20%)
- Cover all major topics
- Include clinical scenarios
- Reference specific literature

### Question Types to Include:
1. **Definition/Concept Questions**
   - Basic principles
   - Terminology
   - Classifications

2. **Clinical Application**
   - Case-based scenarios
   - Treatment decisions
   - Complication management

3. **Surgical Technique**
   - Step-by-step procedures
   - Anatomical landmarks
   - Instrument selection

4. **Evidence-Based Practice**
   - Research findings
   - Guidelines interpretation
   - Comparative outcomes
